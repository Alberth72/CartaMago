# Client Sales Explanation

## Short Pitch

```text
CartaMago convierte su carta en un menú QR que no solo se lee: ayuda al cliente a armar el pedido y enviarlo por WhatsApp de forma ordenada.
```

## What The Restaurant Gets

- A public menu that opens from a QR.
- A practical mobile experience instead of a static PDF.
- Product categories with clear prices.
- A cart that organizes the customer's choice.
- WhatsApp messages with items, quantities, total, delivery mode, name, and notes.
- A fast first launch without a heavy system.

## Why This Can Increase Sales

| Problem today | CartaMago effect |
| --- | --- |
| Customers ask for photos or prices one by one | They see categories and prices immediately |
| WhatsApp orders arrive incomplete | The message arrives structured |
| Staff repeats the same menu information many times | The menu answers common questions first |
| Customer interest gets lost while waiting for a reply | The customer can build the order immediately |
| Printed menus are hard to update | Digital menu can be adjusted faster |
| Promotions are not visible at the moment of purchase | Combos and highlighted items can be placed in the menu |

## Practical Benefits

For customers:

- Less waiting.
- Easier browsing.
- Clearer prices.
- Faster ordering.
- No app installation.

For restaurant staff:

- Fewer messy chats.
- Less manual typing.
- Better order context.
- More time to cook, confirm, and sell.

For the business owner:

- QR can be placed on tables, counter, flyers, delivery bags, and social media.
- Menu becomes a sales channel, not only an information page.
- Launch can happen before investing in a complete ecommerce platform.

## MVP Presentation Script For A Roast Chicken Restaurant

Use this flow in front of the client:

1. Open the QR menu on a phone.
2. Show that the first screen immediately shows food and the restaurant name.
3. Add `Pollo entero asado`.
4. Add `Combo familiar`.
5. Switch between `Recoger`, `Domicilio`, and `Mesa`.
6. Add a customer name and a note.
7. Press `Pedir por WhatsApp`.
8. Show the structured message before sending.

Key phrase:

```text
La diferencia no es solo que el cliente vea el menú. La diferencia es que llega más cerca de comprar.
```

## What Not To Promise Yet

For phase 1, do not promise:

- Automatic order confirmation.
- Payment processing.
- Inventory control.
- Delivery tracking.
- Seller dashboard.
- Multi-branch administration.

Say instead:

```text
Primero validamos que el QR convierta mejor las conversaciones en pedidos. Después agregamos panel, base de datos, pagos o reportes si el flujo demuestra valor.
```

## Recommended Client Language

```text
Este primer paso es liviano: no reemplaza WhatsApp, lo mejora. El cliente arma el pedido y WhatsApp sigue siendo el canal donde usted confirma disponibilidad, tiempo y entrega.
```

```text
Esto ayuda a vender más porque reduce fricción: menos preguntas repetidas, menos pedidos incompletos y más claridad en el momento en que el cliente tiene hambre.
```

## Metrics To Watch

Start simple:

- QR scans or page visits.
- WhatsApp order clicks.
- Most requested products.
- Common customer notes.
- Time saved in chat.
- Number of incomplete orders before and after launch.

Phase 1 can track some metrics manually through WhatsApp. Later, Supabase or analytics can capture them automatically.
