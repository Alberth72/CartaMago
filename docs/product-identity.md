# Product Identity

## Working Name

**CartaMago**

## Meaning

CartaMago is a QR menu that helps a restaurant turn a customer's choice into a ready-to-send WhatsApp order.

The name is intentionally broad. It can serve a roast chicken restaurant today, then a burger shop, bakery, cafe, bar, food truck, or neighborhood store later.

## Positioning

```text
CartaMago convierte un menú escaneado por QR en un pedido listo para WhatsApp.
```

## Tagline Options

Recommended:

```text
Tu carta cobra vida.
```

Alternatives:

```text
Del QR al pedido.
La carta que vende por WhatsApp.
Menús rápidos para vender más.
```

## Naming Shortlist

| Name | Strength | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CartaMago | Memorable, Spanish-first, magical, reusable | Need to verify domain/social availability |
| MenuMago | Very direct and easy | Slightly more generic |
| CartaViva | Elegant and broad | Less clearly tied to ordering |
| QRantojo | Fun and food-oriented | More casual, less premium |
| PideCarta | Clear ordering intent | Less magical |
| MeseroMágico | Strong restaurant metaphor | Longer and more tied to table service |

## Product Promise

For the restaurant:

```text
Recibe pedidos más ordenados por WhatsApp sin instalar sistemas pesados.
```

For the customer:

```text
Escanea, elige y envía tu pedido en segundos.
```

## First Demo Context

Initial vertical:

```text
Asadero de pollos
```

Demo should show:

- Pollos enteros, medios y cuartos.
- Combos familiares.
- Acompañamientos.
- Bebidas.
- Extras.
- Cart summary.
- WhatsApp order handoff.

## Brand Direction

Tone:

- Fast.
- Warm.
- Practical.
- A little magical, without becoming childish.

Visual direction:

- Mobile-first.
- Food-forward.
- High contrast.
- Clear prices.
- Sticky order action.
- No decorative landing page before the menu.
